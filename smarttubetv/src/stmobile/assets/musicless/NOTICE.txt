Musicless feasibility prototype

vocals.onnx and accompaniment.onnx are the Deezer Spleeter 2-stem ONNX exports
from k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx, with ONLY their input/output frame dimension changed
to symbolic by tools/musicless/prepare_models.py (fp32 weights and arithmetic).
